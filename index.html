Main Questline Order

  1. Spawn Introduction
    Talk to NPCs in this exact order in spawn:
    Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick
  2. Travel to Oasis
    After Derrick, teleport to the Oasis using John McDonald.
  3. Oasis Hub
    This is the main hub area where you can:
    • Exchange coins for cosmetics
    • Pick up quests that reward money
  4. Oasis Questline
    Open quests with /quests and go to the Oasis category. You must complete all of Ash’s quests.
  5. PirateBay Unlock
    Completing Ash’s quests unlocks PirateBay. Travel there from the ship near spawn using Captain Snorebeard.
  6. Endgame Hub
    After PirateBay, you’ll gain access to the endgame hub:
    • Island filled with pillagers
    • Fun games & repeatable quests
    • Casino and endgame activities
⚠️ Skipping steps may prevent NPCs from progressing your storyline.

Server Rules

⚠️ Zero Tolerance — Instant Ban, No Warnings

Chat & Conduct

  • Be respectful, helpful, and kind
  • No swearing, hate speech, bullying, threats, or harassment
  • No spam, excessive caps, or attention-seeking behavior
  • No advertising servers, socials, or YouTube
  • English only in public chats
  • Do not mention staff (except reporting SMP downtime)
  • Do not impersonate staff or discuss bans publicly

Gameplay

  • No griefing, stealing, or inappropriate builds
  • Claim land with a golden shovel
  • Don’t touch others’ farms, animals, or claims
  • PvP only if all players agree
  • No exploits, hacks, x-ray, or bug abuse

Staff & Enforcement

  • Follow staff instructions
  • Report issues instead of handling them yourself
  • Lying to staff or bypassing systems is not allowed

Everyone is welcome. We play together, not against each other.
