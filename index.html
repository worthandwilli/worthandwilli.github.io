Main Questline Order

  1. Spawn Introduction
    Talk to NPCs in this exact order in spawn:
    Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick
  2. Travel to Oasis
    After Derrick, teleport to the Oasis using John McDonald.
  3. Oasis Hub
    This is the main hub area where you can:
    • Exchange coins for cosmetics
    • Pick up quests that reward money
  4. Oasis Questline
    Open quests with /quests and go to the Oasis category. You must complete all of Ash’s quests.
  5. PirateBay Unlock
    Completing Ash’s quests unlocks PirateBay. Travel there from the ship near spawn using Captain Snorebeard.
  6. Endgame Hub
    After PirateBay, you’ll gain access to the endgame hub:
    • Island filled with pillagers
    • Fun games & repeatable quests
    • Casino and endgame activities
⚠️ Skipping steps may prevent NPCs from progressing your storyline.

Server Rules

⚠️ Zero Tolerance — Instant Ban, No Warnings

Chat & Communication

  • Be kind, helpful, and respectful.
  • No swearing/bad language, hate speech, bullying, threats, or harassment.
  • No spam or excessive caps.
  • No advertising other servers, social media, or YouTube channels.
  • English only in public chat so everyone can understand.
  • Do not mention staff (only exception: reporting SMP downtime).
  • Do not impersonate staff or “police” other players.
  • Do not discuss bans/past offenses publicly (Minecraft, Discord, or streams).

In-Game Rules

  • No griefing and no stealing.
  • No inappropriate builds (offensive symbols, words, etc.).
  • Don’t build too close to others without permission.
  • Use a golden shovel to claim and protect your land/chests.
  • Don’t touch other players’ farms, animals, or claimed areas.
  • No begging or asking for handouts.

PvP & Exploits

  • PvP only if all players agree (or in BedWars/minigames).
  • No traps, trolling, or tricks that harm others.
  • No hacks, cheats, x-ray, or unauthorized mods.
  • Report bugs/glitches to staff — do not abuse them.

Staff & Enforcement

  • Follow admin/mod instructions.
  • Report rule-breaking instead of trying to handle it yourself.
  • Lying to staff or trying to trick the system is not allowed.

We play together, not against each other. Everyone is welcome — no matter background or skill level.

FAQ

What if this guide doesn’t cover my issue, I find a glitch, or I need admin help? If you encounter a bug/glitch, issues with another player, or need assistance from an admin, the only official way to get proper help is by submitting a new ticket in Discord under Help & Feedback. Personal cases will not be discussed in public/general channels.
How can I earn money in the server? Money is earned mainly through /jobs and certain quests found only in the Oasis and PirateBay areas.
What are Coins / ProCoins? Coins (sometimes called ProCoins) are the currency used to buy cosmetics. They can be earned by a variety of 1400+ quests. They can also be exchanged for normal eco currency.
Why is the exchange rate from Coins to money worse? Because this is not intended to be a main source of income. The economy is meant to be driven by players rather than questing/conversion.
Is Keep Inventory on? No. Keep Inventory is off — but we have Graves that drop headstones. The easiest way to recover your items is /graves tp to teleport back to your gravestone. There is a 48 hour timer to collect your items before they are completely gone.
Why can’t everyone use proximity chat? This is given to our trusted Discord community members that have received a rank of Big Willi or above.
How do I get ranked in Discord? This is only given out by Worth and Willi and it’s recognized by activeness, time, respect, and generally getting to know someone before letting them get close to the family-type vibe. Best to stay active, stay pleasant, and be surprised when it happens. It won’t happen quickly.
Can I be unbanned if I was banned? Unlikely. Bans are usually final. Don’t break the rules.
Can we make factions and rule land / PvP others? No — this is a family-friendly server with limited PvP only mutually agreed upon and/or in BedWars or a minigame.
How do I start the main storyline? Go to spawn and talk to Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick, then use John McDonald to reach the Oasis.
How do I access PirateBay? Finish all of Ash’s Oasis quests via /quests. Captain Snorebeard (ship near spawn) will then teleport you.
Where do I track mcMMO skills? Use /mcmmogui for the GUI.
How do I claim land? Use a golden shovel to create a claim and /trust to add friends.
Why don’t I have my perks? Make sure your Discord is linked and relog. Some perks require specific VIP tiers or Discord boosting.
Where is the live world map? map.wwminecraft.com
