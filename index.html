Pies & Ferris Wheel

🎡 Every 5 Subs = A Ferris Wheel Pie Stop

The Ferris Wheel is now always spinning to keep it more random — you never know who it’s going to land on. When an event triggers it, the wheel stops and whoever it lands on is the result. 🎡

For every 5 total subs (doesn’t matter if it’s one person dropping 5 or 5 different people dropping 1), we trigger a Ferris Wheel stop to decide who gets a pie to the face.

The color of the top Ferris wheel pod decides the victim:

  • Pink Pod – Mom gets a pie. 🥧
  • Blue Pod – Willi gets a pie. 🥧
  • Red Pod – Dad gets a pie. 🥧
  • Green Pod – The last user to donate a sub chooses who gets the pie.

🎁 Special: 10 Gifted Subs = 3 Pies

If someone drops 10 subs in one gift bomb, that counts as 3 pies instead of just 2.

We trigger the Ferris Wheel stop once:

  • If it lands on Pink, Blue, or Red, whoever that color belongs to has to take all 3 pies. 🥧🥧🥧
  • If it lands on Green, the user who gifted the 10 subs gets to choose who takes the 3 pies.

Big sub bombs = big mess. We regret everything already. 😅
