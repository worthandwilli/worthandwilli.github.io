FAQ

What if this guide doesn’t cover my issue, I find a glitch, or I need admin help? If you encounter a bug, glitch, issue with another player, or need assistance from an admin, the only official way to receive proper help is by submitting a new ticket in Discord under Help & Feedback. Personal cases will not be discussed in public or general channels.
How can I earn money in the server? Money is primarily earned through /jobs and by completing certain quests found only in the Oasis and PirateBay areas.
What are Coins / ProCoins? Coins (sometimes called ProCoins) are used to purchase cosmetics. They are earned through a variety of 1400+ quests and can also be exchanged for normal eco currency.
Why is the exchange rate from Coins to money so bad? Because this exchange is not intended to be a main source of income. The economy is designed to be player-driven (jobs, shops, trading), not focused on converting quest currency.
Is Keep Inventory on? No. Keep Inventory is off. However, the server uses a Graves system. When you die, a gravestone is created that stores your items. You can use /graves tp to teleport back to your gravestone. Items must be collected within 48 hours, or they will be permanently removed.
Why can’t everyone use proximity chat? Proximity chat is reserved for trusted Discord community members who have received the rank of Big Willi or above.
How do I get ranked in Discord? Discord ranks are only granted by Worth and Willi. They are based on activeness, time, respect, and genuinely getting to know someone. Best advice: stay active, stay pleasant, and be surprised when it happens. It does not happen quickly.
Can I be unbanned if I was banned? Unlikely. Bans are typically final. The best way to avoid this is simple: follow the rules.
Can we create factions and PvP other players? No. This is a family-friendly server. PvP is limited to mutually agreed fights, BedWars, or minigames.
How do I start the main storyline? Go to spawn and speak with Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick, then use John McDonald to reach the Oasis.
Where is the live world map? map.wwminecraft.com
