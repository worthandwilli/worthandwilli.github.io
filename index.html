Welcome to the Worth & Willi Minecraft Server. This guide explains the main questline order, helpful commands, supporter perks, and how to use the live world map.

Main Questline Order

  1. Spawn Introduction
    Speak to NPCs in this exact order:
    Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick
  2. Travel to Oasis
    After Derrick, teleport using John McDonald.
  3. Oasis Hub
    Exchange coins for cosmetics and pick up quests that reward money.
  4. Oasis Quest Category
    Open /quests → Oasis category → complete all of Ash’s quests.
  5. PirateBay Unlock
    After completing Ash’s quests, speak with Captain Snorebeard on the ship near spawn.
  6. Endgame Hub
    Unlock the endgame island filled with pillagers, fun games, quests, and a casino.
⚠️ Skipping steps may prevent NPCs from progressing your storyline.
