FAQ

Worthathon Questions

What happens if no one adds any time?

The stream ends Friday at 8:00 AM ET after the guaranteed 12 hours.

Can the stream go past 24 hours?

Only if the community reaches the full 24-hour goal. Dad then adds 2 bonus hours, with an absolute hard stop at Friday 10:00 PM ET.

What happens when we reach the full 24-hour goal?

Dad automatically adds 2 more hours. That makes the maximum Worthathon 26 hours total, ending Friday at 10:00 PM ET. The stream has to stop at that point.

Do gifted subs count?

Yes. Each gifted sub adds 3 minutes 36 seconds.

How do Bits work?

Every 100 Bits adds 36 seconds. That means 500 Bits adds 3 minutes and 1,000 Bits adds 6 minutes.

What happens if Dad falls asleep?

Overnight support can trigger Nightmare Mode: Bits blast the lights bright and play loud sound alerts, while selected gifted-sub and donation amounts trigger music or prank audio.

Why do direct tips add more time?

Direct tips are intentionally the best timer value: every $1 adds 1 minute 12 seconds.
