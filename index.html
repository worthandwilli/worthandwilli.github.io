Main Questline Order

  1. Spawn Introduction
    Speak with NPCs in this exact order at spawn:
    Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick
  2. Travel to Oasis
    After Derrick, teleport using John McDonald.
  3. Oasis Hub
    The Oasis is the main progression hub where you can:
    • Exchange Coins / ProCoins for cosmetics
    • Pick up quests that reward money
  4. Oasis Questline
    Open /quests and complete all quests in the Oasis category. All of Ash’s quests must be completed.
  5. PirateBay Unlock
    Completing Ash’s quests unlocks PirateBay. Use Captain Snorebeard on the ship near spawn.
  6. Endgame Hub
    After PirateBay you’ll gain access to the endgame hub:
    • Pillager-filled island
    • Endgame quests & activities
    • Minigames and casino
⚠️ Skipping steps may prevent NPCs from progressing your storyline.

Server Rules

⚠️ Zero Tolerance — Instant Ban, No Warnings

Chat & Conduct

  • Be respectful, helpful, and kind
  • No swearing, hate speech, bullying, harassment, or threats
  • No spam, excessive caps, or attention-seeking behavior
  • No advertising servers or social media
  • English only in public chats
  • Do not mention staff unless reporting SMP downtime
  • Do not impersonate staff or discuss bans publicly

Gameplay

  • No griefing, stealing, or inappropriate builds
  • Claim land using a golden shovel
  • No touching others’ farms, animals, or claims
  • PvP only by mutual agreement or in minigames
  • No hacks, cheats, x-ray, or bug abuse

Staff & Enforcement

  • Follow staff instructions
  • Report issues via Discord tickets
  • Lying to staff or bypassing systems is not allowed

We play together, not against each other. Everyone is welcome.

FAQ

Is Keep Inventory on? No. Keep Inventory is off. We use a Graves system that creates a headstone on death. Use /graves tp to teleport back. Items must be collected within 48 hours.
How do I earn money? Through /jobs and quests found in Oasis and PirateBay.
What are Coins / ProCoins? Cosmetic currency earned from 1400+ quests. Can be exchanged for eco money, but not intended as a main income source.
Why can’t everyone use proximity chat? It’s reserved for trusted Discord members ranked Big Willi or higher.
Can I be unbanned? Unlikely. Bans are usually final.
Can we make factions or PvP freely? No. This is a family-friendly server with limited PvP.
