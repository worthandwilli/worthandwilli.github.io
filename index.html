Main Questline Order

  1. Spawn Intro: Speak to Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick
  2. Travel to Oasis: Use John McDonald
  3. Oasis Hub: Cosmetics exchange + money quests
  4. Oasis Quests: /quests → complete all of Ash’s quests
  5. PirateBay: Unlock via Captain Snorebeard near spawn
  6. Endgame Hub: Pillagers, games, quests, and casino
⚠️ Quest steps must be completed in order or NPCs may not progress.

Server Rules

⚠️ Zero Tolerance Zone – Instant Ban, No Warnings

Chat & Conduct

  • Be respectful, kind, and helpful
  • No swearing, hate speech, harassment, bullying, or threats
  • No spam, excessive caps, or attention-seeking behavior
  • No advertising other servers or social media
  • English only in public chats
  • Do not mention staff unless reporting the SMP being down
  • Do not impersonate staff or police other players
  • Do not discuss bans or past offenses publicly

Gameplay Rules

  • No griefing, stealing, or inappropriate builds
  • Claim land using a golden shovel
  • Do not touch others’ farms, animals, or claims
  • No begging for items or handouts
  • PvP only if all players agree
  • No traps, trolling, exploits, or bug abuse

Staff & Enforcement

  • Follow staff instructions
  • Report issues instead of handling them yourself
  • Lying to staff or bypassing systems is not allowed
  • Accounts closely associated with banned players may also be removed

We play together, not against each other. Everyone is welcome.

Client-Side Mods Policy

✅ Allowed (Non-Performance / Cosmetic)

  • AppleSkin, Iris (Shaders), Distant Horizons
  • Litematica, Lighty, Minimap / World Map mods
  • FullBright, Zoom mods
  • Inventory & UI enhancements
  • Fresh Animations, 3D Skin Layers

🚫 Prohibited (Any Variant)

  • X-ray, cheats, hacks, or exploit mods
  • Auto-AFK, auto-clickers, macros
  • Auto reconnect, auto logout
  • Freecam, ReplayMod
  • Baritone or AI-based automation
⚠️ Unauthorized mods may result in warnings, kicks, or bans.
