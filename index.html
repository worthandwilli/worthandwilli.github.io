Main Questline Order

  1. Spawn Introduction
    Talk to NPCs in this exact order in spawn:
    Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick
  2. Travel to Oasis
    After Derrick, teleport to the Oasis using John McDonald.
  3. Oasis Hub
    This is the main hub area where you can:
    • Exchange coins for cosmetics
    • Pick up quests that reward money
  4. Oasis Questline
    Open quests with /quests and go to the Oasis category. You must complete all of Ash’s quests.
  5. PirateBay Unlock
    Completing Ash’s quests unlocks PirateBay. Travel there from the ship near spawn using Captain Snorebeard.
  6. Endgame Hub
    After PirateBay, you’ll gain access to the endgame hub:
    • Island filled with pillagers
    • Fun games & repeatable quests
    • Casino and endgame activities
⚠️ Skipping steps may prevent NPCs from progressing your storyline.

FAQ

What if this guide doesn’t cover my issue, I find a glitch, or I need admin help? If you encounter a bug/glitch, issues with another player, or need assistance from an admin, the only official way to get proper help is by submitting a new ticket in Discord under Help & Feedback. Personal cases will not be discussed in public/general channels.
How can I earn money in the server? Money is earned mainly through /jobs and through certain quests found only in the Oasis and PirateBay areas.
What are Coins / ProCoins? Coins (sometimes called ProCoins) are the server currency used to buy cosmetics. They can be earned through a variety of 1400+ quests, and they can also be exchanged for normal eco currency.
Why is the exchange rate from Coins to money so bad? Because this exchange is not intended to be your main source of income. The main economy is meant to be driven by players (jobs, trading, shops), not by converting coins from questing.
Why can’t everyone use proximity chat? Proximity chat is a perk given to our trusted Discord community members who have received the rank of Big Willi or above.
How do I get ranked in Discord? Ranks are only given out by Worth and Willi. They are based on activeness, time, respect, and truly getting to know someone before allowing a closer “family vibe” connection. Best advice: stay active, stay pleasant, and be surprised when it happens. It won’t happen quickly.
Can I be unbanned if I was banned? Unlikely. Bans are usually final. The best way to stay is simple: don’t break the rules.
Can we make factions, claim land, and PvP other players? No. This is a family-friendly server with limited PvP. PvP is only allowed by mutual agreement, or within BedWars / minigames.
How do I start the main storyline? Go to spawn and talk to Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick, then use John McDonald to reach the Oasis.
How do I access PirateBay? Finish all of Ash’s Oasis quests in the Oasis category via /quests. Then Captain Snorebeard (ship near spawn) can teleport you.
Where do I track mcMMO skills? Use /mcmmogui for the GUI.
How do I claim land? Use a golden shovel to create a claim and /trust to add friends.
Why don’t I have my perks? Make sure your Discord is linked and relog. Some perks require specific VIP tiers.
