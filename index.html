Main Questline (Do This In Order)

  1. Spawn Intro
    Talk to NPCs in this order in spawn:
    Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick
  2. Travel to Oasis
    After Derrick, teleport to the Oasis using:
    John McDonald
  3. Oasis Hub
    You’ll arrive at the main hub area where you can:
    • Exchange coins for cosmetics
    • Pick up quests that reward money
  4. Oasis Quest Category
    Open quests with /quests and go to the Oasis category.
    Complete all of Ash’s quests.
  5. Unlock PirateBay
    After completing Ash’s Oasis quests, you gain access to PirateBay.
    Travel there from the ship near spawn using:
    Captain Snorebeard

    Captain Snorebeard only teleports players who finished the required Oasis quests.

  6. Unlock Endgame Hub
    Once PirateBay requirements are complete, you can be teleported to the endgame hub:
    • Island filled with pillagers
    • Fun games & repeatable quests
    • A casino
Important: If you skip steps, NPCs may not progress your storyline. Do them in order.

FAQ

How do I start the main storyline?

Go to spawn and talk to Queen → Mag → Bear → Derrick, then use John McDonald to reach the Oasis.

How do I access PirateBay?

Finish all of Ash’s Oasis quests in the Oasis category via /quests. Then Captain Snorebeard (ship near spawn) can teleport you.

How do I claim land?

Use the GriefPrevention claim tools/commands (see the Commands section: claim, trust, abandon, etc.).

Where do I track mcMMO skills?

Use /mcmmogui for the nice GUI (and /mcmmo for basics if enabled).
